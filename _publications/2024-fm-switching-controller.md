This paper addresses the synthesis of switching controllers for hybrid systems whose objectives are given by a fragment of Signal Temporal Logic (STL), which essentially corresponds to reach-avoid problems with timing constraints. The approach iteratively computes the state sets that can be driven to satisfy the specification, supports both constant and non-constant hybrid dynamics, and is proved sound with relative completeness for a subclass of hybrid systems.


**Authors:** Han Su, Shenghua Feng, Simon Sinong Zhan, Naijun Zhan
