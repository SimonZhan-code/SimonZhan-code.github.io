Reinforcement learning enables adversaries to break safety alignment of LLMs more effectively than supervised fine-tuning. TokenBuncher is the first defense specifically targeting RL-based harmful fine-tuning: it suppresses model response entropy — the foundation RL exploits — through entropy-as-reward RL and a Token Noiser mechanism, preventing escalation of harmful capabilities while preserving benign utility.


**Authors:** Weitao Feng, Lixu Wang, Tianyi Wei, Jie Zhang, Chongyang Gao, Simon Sinong Zhan, Peizhuo Lv, Wei Dong
