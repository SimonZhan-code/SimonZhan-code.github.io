Reinforcement Learning with Verified Reward (RLVR) is a central paradigm for advancing LLM reasoning. This work introduces a unified clipping framework that characterizes existing GRPO-style methods through a general notion of policy divergence, encompassing both likelihood ratios and KL divergences and extending to alternative measures, and identifies the variance-reduced KL3 Monte Carlo estimator as a key policy divergence constraint.


**Authors:** Qingyuan Wu, Yuhui Wang, Simon Sinong Zhan, Yanning Dai, Shilong Deng, Sarra Habchi, Qi Zhu, Matthias Gallé, Chao Huang
