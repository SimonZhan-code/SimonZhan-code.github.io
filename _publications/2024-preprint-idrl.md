This work introduces an inverse reinforcement learning framework designed to extract rewarding features from expert trajectories affected by delayed disturbances. It employs an efficient off-policy adversarial training scheme to derive expert features and recover optimal policies from augmented delayed observations, validated in MuJoCo environments under diverse delay settings.


**Authors:** Simon Sinong Zhan*, Qingyuan Wu*, Zhian Ruan, Frank Yang, Philip Wang, Yixuan Wang, Ruochen Jiao, Chao Huang, Qi Zhu (*equal contribution)
